# pressure-test changelog

Earlier history lives in the repo-level CHANGELOG.md.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-20

- An observable or empirical question is answered by running a spike, not by asking the user.
- Spikes are bounded: no paid services, no writes to shared or production systems, no publishing; scratch directory outside the source tree, never committed.
- Do not invent speculative implementation risks for an unbuilt plan; spike the risks that turn on observable behavior. The decision spine still applies to plans on paper. Sous mode unchanged.
- Adapted from pstack's prototype playbook and design guide by Lauren Tan (MIT).
